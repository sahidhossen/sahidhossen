Support

Questions, bugs and feature requests are welcome.

Email [support email]. Please include your Jira site address and, for bugs, the steps that lead to the problem. Do not send issue contents or personal data unless they are needed to understand the problem.

Response times

Support is provided in English and German, Monday to Friday, Central European Time. Most questions get an answer within two business days.

Security

To report a security issue, email the same address with "Security" in the subject.
