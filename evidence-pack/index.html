Change evidence your auditor can check

Evidence Pack reads the history of your Jira tickets and shows, change by change, whether it was approved, approved before work started, and approved by someone other than the person who did it. Then it hands you the result as a PDF your auditor can re-check.

What it replaces

When an auditor asks for "the full history of these 20 changes", most teams spend days on screenshots and spreadsheet exports. Evidence Pack builds the same evidence from the system of record in about a minute, picks the sample with a recorded seed so nothing looks cherry-picked, and lists every exception with its reason.

What you get

  • Evidence packs. Pick changes with JQL and a period, test all of them or a random sample, and download a PDF and CSV with a SHA-256 fingerprint.
  • Approval in the ticket. Approvers approve or reject right on the Jira issue. Each decision is chained to the previous one, so later edits show up.
  • Emergency changes. Changes with an emergency label may be approved within a window after work starts, as most change policies allow.
  • Code evidence. Optionally require a linked pull request, commit, deployment or link for every change.
  • Control mapping. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A 8.32, SOC 2 CC8.1, DORA Art. 9(4)(e) and NIS2 Art. 21(2)(e).
  • A 12-month trend. See how often changes miss the rules and what the most common causes are.
  • Monthly packs. Last month's pack is built on the first of every month and stored in the app.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. Evidence Pack runs entirely on Atlassian's own infrastructure and stores its records inside your Atlassian site. It has no servers of its own and sends no data to any outside service. The developer has no access to your data. See the privacy policy.
