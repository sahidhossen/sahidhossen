Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Evidence Pack app for Jira Cloud processes, where it is stored and who can access it.

Who we are

[your name or business name], [street, postcode, city, country]. Contact: [support email].

The short version

  • The app runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform.
  • Everything it stores stays inside your Atlassian site.
  • It sends no data to us or to any third party.
  • We have no access to your Jira data or to the app's stored data.

Roles

Your organisation is the controller of the data in its Jira site. Atlassian hosts and runs the app on its infrastructure as part of Forge, under Atlassian's own privacy policy and data processing terms. Because no data reaches us, we do not process your personal data when the app runs.

What the app reads

To build packs and show approval status, the app reads from Jira: issue keys, summaries, types, labels, reporters, assignees, the history of statuses, assignees and labels (including who made each change and when), development summaries and links. If you turn on Jira Service Management approvals, it also reads the approval steps of service requests, including who approved or declined. It reads Jira user names and group memberships to show who approved and to apply your access settings.

All of this is stored in Atlassian-hosted app storage that belongs to your site.

How long it is kept

Data is kept until your organisation uninstalls the app. Atlassian then removes the app's storage according to its Forge data retention rules. Admins can export all stored data at any time from the app's Settings.

Closed Atlassian accounts

The app reports the account ids it stores to Atlassian's personal data reporting service every week. When Atlassian reports that an account has been closed, the app removes that person's name and account id from approval records. The integrity chain remains verifiable because it is built on a one-way hash of the account id.

Your rights

Requests to access, correct or erase personal data in your Jira site should go to your organisation, which controls that data. Atlassian account holders can also manage their data through Atlassian.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data handling changes and note the date at the top.
