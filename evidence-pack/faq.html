Frequently asked questions

Does Evidence Pack send my data anywhere?

No. It runs on Atlassian's Forge platform, stores everything inside your Atlassian site, and makes no calls to outside services.

Can the developer see our data?

No. There is no developer-run server or database. The developer cannot access your Jira or the app's stored data.

Which Jira permissions does it ask for and why?

PermissionWhy
Read Jira workRead issues and their status history to build packs.
Read Jira usersShow who approved and who implemented, and check group membership.
Write Jira workAdd a comment and optionally move the issue when someone approves in the panel.
App storageStore settings, packs and approval records inside your site.

Whose permissions are used to read issues?

Packs you build in the app read issues as you, so you only see what you can see in Jira. Background and monthly packs run as the app.

How large can a pack be?

Up to 2,000 changes. Packs with more than 100 changes are built in the background; you can leave the page and open the result from History.

Does it work with team-managed and company-managed projects?

Yes. It only relies on statuses and issue history, which both project types have.

Can I change a pack after it is generated?

No. Packs are stored as generated, with a SHA-256 fingerprint. To include more changes, generate a new pack.

Is Evidence Pack available in German?

Yes. The app follows each user's Jira language, and you can choose English or German for each PDF.

What happens when we uninstall?

The app's stored data is removed by Atlassian according to its Forge data retention rules. Export your data first if you need to keep it.
